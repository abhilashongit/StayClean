Outcomes

01

Decide once. It holds.

Rules you set in daylight, kept at night.

d = 0.65medium-to-large effect on reaching goalsGollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006
Evidence
The study

A meta-analysis of 94 independent tests found that deciding in advance what you will do in a given situation helps people reach their goals.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006), Advances in Experimental Social Psychology 38. Source ↗
In Stay Clean

Your 12–5 am rule is a plan made while calm and enforced while tired.

02

Hard to undo on impulse.

A speed bump built for a weak moment.

+3 ptshigher quit success, still there a year laterGiné, Karlan & Zinman, 2010
Evidence
The study

In a randomized trial of about 2,000 smokers, those offered a voluntary commitment contract were around 3 percentage points more likely to pass a 6-month test, and the effect held at 12 months. Only 11% took the offer up.

Giné, Karlan & Zinman (2010), American Economic Journal: Applied Economics. Source ↗
In Stay Clean

Device Admin makes uninstalling a deliberate step, and the settings pages stay shut overnight.

03

Fall asleep without the scroll.

Instagram and X rest from 11 pm to 5 am.

3×odds of disturbed sleep for the most frequent checkersLevenson et al., 2016
Evidence
The study

Among 1,788 US adults aged 19–32, the most frequent social media checkers had about three times the odds of sleep disturbance versus the least frequent.

Levenson et al. (2016), Preventive Medicine 85. Source ↗ An association, not proof of cause.
In Stay Clean

04

Scroll time that is yours.

Instagram opens 5 minutes in every 30.

3 wksof capped use, less loneliness and depressionHunt et al., 2018
Evidence
The study

143 students limited Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat to 10 minutes each per day. After three weeks they reported significantly lower loneliness and depression than the control group.

Hunt et al. (2018), Journal of Social and Clinical Psychology. Source ↗
In Stay Clean

The 5-in-30 pacing is a gentler cap than the one in this study, so treat it as the same idea, not the same result.

Why Stay Clean

Set it once. Then forget it.

The best habit tool is the one that asks nothing of you. Tools that wait for you to switch them on need you at the very moment you have the least willpower.

Under the hood
Event-driven, never pollingAn AccessibilityService subscribed to TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED only. Android delivers the events, so the app never loops waiting for them.
Scoped by packageBy day the event filter narrows to 17 packages: 13 browsers, Swiggy, Zomato, Instagram and X. The system never dispatches events for anything else.
Phase-gatedThree IST phases: DAY, TAMPER, ACTIVE. The foreground watchdog exists only in ACTIVE and stops itself the moment it ends.
One exact alarm at a timesetExactAndAllowWhileIdle aims at the next boundary, with a 5-minute heartbeat at night. Boot and clock-change receivers re-arm it.
No wake locksInternal timers use uptime, so they run only while the phone is already awake and never pull it out of deep sleep.
Decisions stay localA small pure-Java rule table in memory. The only network call is a tiny time check at boot or when the clock changes.

The guard does not depend on charge level: a block is decided on the phone and drawn straight from the event, with no network lookup. On phones with aggressive battery managers, set Stay Clean to Unrestricted in setup so the system does not put it to sleep.

Reference: Android vitals treats more than 2 cumulative hours of wake locks in 24 hours as excessive. Android Developers Blog ↗

Install

On your phone in two minutes.

Stay Clean is for Android. It is not on iPhone, and not in the Play Store yet, so you install it directly from GitHub.

● Android 8.0 and up○ iPhone not supported
  1. Download the APKOpen the latest release on your phone and tap stay-clean-v1.3.apk.
  2. Allow the installIf Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps.
  3. Choose “Install anyway”Play Protect may warn because the app is new and uses Accessibility. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Finish the six setup rowsDo them top to bottom and do Guard last. If Guard is greyed out: Settings, Apps, Stay Clean, ⋮, Allow restricted settings.

Questions

Good to know.

No. There is no account, no analytics and no server. The only network request is a tiny time check, so the app can tell a real clock from a changed one. The code is open for anyone to read.

It is built as a speed bump for a weak moment, not a prison. Some settings are held shut at night, and it never locks you out of your own phone. A determined person can still remove it by day. That is the honest trade-off.

It uses the Accessibility permission to see which app is open, and it is installed outside the Play Store. Android and Play Protect are cautious about that combination. The source and a checksum are published so you can verify it.

Swiggy and Zomato overnight, Instagram and X at night if you switch on the Night lock, Instagram pacing all day, and adult sites in Chrome and other popular browsers.
